SaleeM 3.20.0 — Distant Limit Recommendations

Modified files:
- app/services/analyzer.py
  Generates two dynamic, manual pending-order plans after each live analysis:
  Buy Limit below current price and Sell Limit above current price.
  Each plan includes an entry zone, exact entry, distant stop, TP1/TP2/TP3,
  distance from current price, and an estimated non-guaranteed percentage.
- app/templates/index.html
  Adds a collapsed "توصية الصفقة" panel directly below

- app/static/style.css

- app/main.py

- app/__init__.py
  Version updated to 3.20.0.
- tests/test_limit_recommendations_v320.py
  Tests distant entries, stop/target geometry, disclaimer, inactive market,
  and panel placement.
