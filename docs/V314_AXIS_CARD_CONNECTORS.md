# SaleeM v3.14 — Axis card connectors

- True price lines remain fixed at the exact `price_to_y` result.
- All right-axis cards, including R1/R2/S1/S2, share one width, height, radius and typography.
- Card prices use one decimal place.
- Overlapping cards move vertically only inside the right price-axis column.
- Each displaced card receives a thin colored connector from its immutable true price line to the card center.
- Support/resistance line width still increases with strength.
- No price value or analytical level is changed by collision handling.
