# SaleeM v3.49 — True anchored overlays

1. The uploaded chart remains the background and is never rebuilt.
2. Price Y uses the strict pixel-derived broker axis.
3. Candle X is calibrated by matching visible screenshot wick high/low and candle direction against recent M5 market candles.
4. If candle X alignment is untrusted, pattern/structure/zone X-linked overlays are hidden rather than guessed.
5. W/M and other pattern paths are drawn only through their real swing anchors.
6. BOS/CHOCH require a real close through a prior swing level and span from the broken swing candle to the breaking candle.
7. IDM is only the last real internal opposite swing within the current BOS leg.
8. OB/FVG begin at their detected candle and retain their exact price top/bottom.
9. Confirmed patterns may show Entry Zone, Neckline/Activation, Invalidation and a measured forecast arrow starting at the real breakout candle.
10. Labels may move for readability; anchors, levels, zones and geometry never move for cosmetic reasons.
